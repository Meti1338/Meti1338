Thornwood Skirmish

Aruna, Ayo and Serafina are ambushed on the road out of the academy.

  • Break: hit a weakness to remove a shield. At zero the enemy is Broken, loses a turn and takes +50% damage.
  • Boost: you earn 1 BP each round. Spend up to 3 on a turn for more hits or power.
  • Defend: during enemy attacks, act as the ring closes. Gold = PARRY, blue = DODGE.
  • Press Space, tap the scene, or use the Defend button.
