Thornwood Clearing

Aruna, Ayo and Serafina are ambushed by the forest's awakened guardians.

  • Break: hit weaknesses to strip shields. At zero the enemy is stunned and takes +50% damage.
  • Boost: earn 1 BP per round. Press E/Q or +/− to spend up to 3 for more hits and bigger spells.
  • Defend: on enemy turns press Space or tap as the rings meet. Gold rings: parry. Blue rings: dodge.
